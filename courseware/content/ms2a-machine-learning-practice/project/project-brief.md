# The Brief

The project is the grade of this course, and it is one challenge:
**DS-Harness**. You build an AI system around a frugal LLM so that
it answers data-science objectives it could never answer alone.

## How you are graded, in one slide

| Part | Share of the project |

## Pairs, and the rules that matter

- Teams of **two**, created on the
  [challenge page](https://ml-arena.com/viewchallenge/194) tab teams, declared by **2026-10-15**
- **10 deployments per person per rolling 24 h**, across every ML-Arena
  challenge, failed ones included: test locally first
- Choose the **PyTorch** runtime when you submit .

## The oral and presentation

10 minutes of presentation, 10 minutes of questions.

| | Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | Problem statement and methodology | 20 % |
| O2 | System design | 30 % |
| O3 | Evaluation methodology | 30 % |
| O4 | Best solution clearly stated | 10 % |
| O5 | Failure analysis and next steps | 10 % |

Slides as a PDF, 15 slides at most.

Send your team's PDF, `$team_name.pdf`, to raphaelcousin.education@gmail.com
with the subject `ms2a 2026-11-04 $team_name`.
